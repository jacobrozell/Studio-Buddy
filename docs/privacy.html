Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Studio Buddy is designed to keep your music and data private.

What we collect

Your catalog and audio stay on your device. Release builds send allowlisted usage events through Firebase Analytics (Google Analytics 4) and diagnostic reports through Firebase Crashlytics. Those payloads never include song titles, file names, notes, artist names, or audio content.

Debug builds do not send analytics unless you launch with -enable_analytics. Launching with -disable_analytics turns remote telemetry off.

Where your data lives

The audio files you import and the metadata you create (songs, mixes, projects, BPM/key, notes) are stored only on your device, inside the app's private storage. They are included in your device/iCloud backups if you have those enabled, under Apple's terms.

Sharing

When you use a share action (text, tracklist, or a release card image), content is handed to the system share sheet and goes only to the destination you choose. Studio Buddy does not see or retain it.

Microphone

If a future version uses the microphone to record audio, iOS will ask your permission first, and recordings are stored on-device like any other audio.

Children

Studio Buddy does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
